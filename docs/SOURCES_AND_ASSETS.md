## Building atlas contribution policy

Every building package must separate importable data, contributor-owned material, and reference-only evidence. Record the stable building ID, source URL or file ID, retrieval/observation date, licence, supported claim, confidence, reviewer, and any privacy or consent decision. Do not describe an inferred façade, tenant, floor plan, or interior as surveyed fact.

Code, geographic databases, photographs, textures, and authored models remain separate licence layers. New authored building models should target CC BY 4.0 or CC0 only when the contributor can grant those rights cleanly. Preserve ODbL, CC BY-SA, attribution, notice, and other source obligations rather than relabeling a combined package. See [BUILDING_CONTRIBUTION_GUIDE.md](BUILDING_CONTRIBUTION_GUIDE.md) for the required package fields and review checklist.

`src/player/PlayerAvatar.ts` loads the contemporary rigged player. `src/life/PedestrianDetail.ts` swaps one nearby adult from the instanced crowd for a detailed male or female character, while `src/life/PedestrianRig.ts` and `tools/bpl/gen_people.py` keep the background instanced and add facial geometry. The detailed models improve close views but are still stylized game characters rather than photoreal humans. The two NPC model files total about 24 MB and both are loaded at startup; one is drawn for the nearby NPC at a time. Asset variants need their own provenance and redistribution check.

The detailed NPC pass currently represents only two outfits and a single close-range pedestrian at once. A future character task should add more licensed appearance variants and check model-loading cost and performance on slower hardware before raising that limit. Keep the background crowd instanced. Record whether each new character was downloaded, manually made, or generated, along with its exact model, texture, and animation licences.

The two enterable interiors are code-built hero scenes in `src/interiors/`. They are not a reusable inventory of surveyed SF interiors. New interiors should prioritize public, commercial, institutional, or owner-approved spaces and record their access/consent basis. A visible entrance or listing photo is not evidence of an accurate or reusable floor plan. Logos/storefront names identify real businesses but are not licensed game art by virtue of appearing in map data. Do not bundle third-party photos, meshes, character models, proprietary street-view exports, or listing media without explicit reuse rights.
